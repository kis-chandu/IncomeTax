# Verified Research — FY 2025-26 / AY 2026-27 Filing Season
*Compiled 29 Jul 2026. Every fact below confirmed via web sources listed at the end. No assumptions.*

## A. Deadlines (AY 2026-27)
- ITR-1 / ITR-2 (non-audit individuals): **31 Jul 2026**. No extension notified as of 21 Jul 2026.
- ITR-3 / ITR-4 non-audit: **31 Aug 2026** (new permanent 1-month extension from this year; Finance Act 2026).
- Tax audit cases: 31 Oct 2026 | Transfer pricing: 30 Nov 2026.
- Belated: 31 Dec 2026 — fee ₹1,000 (income ≤₹5L) / ₹5,000 (>₹5L); interest u/s 234A; most losses forfeited.
- Revised: **31 Mar 2027** (extended from 31 Dec by Budget 2026). Updated (ITR-U): up to 31 Mar 2031 (48 months from end of AY; extra tax 25–70%).

## B. Which law governs
- Income-tax Act, 2025 in force from **1 Apr 2026**, applies to income earned **FY 2026-27 onwards**; introduces single "Tax Year" concept (536 sections; old FY/AY replaced).
- **FY 2025-26 (AY 2026-27) returns — now being filed — remain entirely under the Income-tax Act, 1961.** Old section numbers (80C, 87A, 24b…) apply this season.
- ITR forms AY 2026-27 notified under the Income-tax Rules, 1962; forms carry dual old+new section references during transition.

## C. Slabs FY 2025-26
**New regime (default):** 0–4L nil | 4–8L 5% | 8–12L 10% | 12–16L 15% | 16–20L 20% | 20–24L 25% | >24L 30%.
- Std deduction ₹75,000 (salaried/pensioners). 87A rebate ₹60,000 → **zero tax at taxable income ≤₹12L (≈₹12.75L gross salary)**.
- Marginal relief exists just above ₹12L (tax capped at income exceeding ₹12L, computed on slab-rate income).
- Surcharge: >₹50L 10%, >₹1Cr 15%, >₹2Cr capped 25% (new). +4% HEC always.
**Old regime (opt-in):** 0–2.5L nil | 2.5–5L 5% | 5–10L 20% | >10L 30% (<60 yrs; senior 3L/super-senior 5L basic exemption).
- Std deduction ₹50,000; 87A rebate ₹12,500 at taxable ≤₹5L. Full deductions Ch VIA, HRA, 24(b).
- **New-regime survivors:** employer NPS 80CCD(2) @14% of Basic+DA, Agniveer 80CCH, employer gratuity/leave encashment exemptions, etc.

## D. ITR form changes AY 2026-27
- ITR-1 & ITR-4: now allow **two house properties** (was one).
- ITR-1: can report **LTCG u/s 112A up to ₹1.25L**; NOT for directors, NRIs, unlisted-share holders, foreign assets, business income.
- New disclosures: unrealised/arrears rent, 80G transaction reference, 80GGC political-party details (name+PAN), more financial particulars in ITR-4, AIS-mismatch declarations in ITR-2/3/4.
- Relief u/s 89A (foreign retirement accounts) removed from ITR-1/ITR-4 → requires ITR-2/3.
- Pre/post-Budget-2024 capital-gains split removed (uniform rates all FY 2025-26).
- Filing triggers even below exemption: TDS >₹25k, current a/c deposits >₹1Cr, foreign travel spend >₹2L, electricity >₹1L etc.

## E. Capital gains (FY 2025-26 rates) — client has none this year; retained for reference
- Equity STCG (111A): **20%**. Equity LTCG (112A): **12.5% above ₹1.25L/yr** exemption. Grandfathering FMV 31-Jan-2018 for pre-2018 equity.
- Other LTCG (112): **12.5% no indexation**; land/building acquired **before 23-Jul-2024** — resident individual/HUF may choose **20% with indexation if lower** (grandfathering).
- Debt MFs bought ≥1-Apr-2023: always slab rate (deemed ST). STCG non-equity: slab rate.
- Set-off: ST loss vs any CG; LT loss only vs LTCG; carry forward 8 years **only if ITR filed by due date**.

## F. Section 87A rebate vs special-rate income (KEY TRAP)
- **CBDT Circular 13/2025: 87A rebate NOT available against tax on special-rate income (111A STCG, 112A LTCG) under the new regime**; CPC raising demands for earlier wrongly-allowed claims (interest u/s 220(2) waived if paid by 31 Dec 2025 — past now).
- Some CIT(A) orders + Bombay HC (Chamber of Tax Consultants, Jan 2025) favoured taxpayers for AY 2024-25; department position controls portal/utility behaviour. **Prudent filing: claim rebate only on slab-rate income in new regime.**
- Old regime: rebate allowed on total income incl. STCG (111A), but §112A LTCG excluded per §112A(6).

## G. Compliance mechanics
- AIS/TIS/26AS reconciliation mandatory — mismatches = automated notices. SB interest, dividends, rent, securities all pre-reported.
- Returns not **e-verified within 30 days** = treated as not filed.
- Refund only to **pre-validated** bank account.
- Employer’s TDS regime assumption (Form 16) **does not bind** taxpayer at filing; choose either regime (salaried can switch every year).

## H. Interest & dividend TDS (verified 29 Jul 2026)
- **Bond/listed debenture interest (Sec 193)**: TDS 10% (20% without PAN); Budget 2025 threshold ₹10,000/FY; fully taxable at slab as IFOS — **TDS is not final tax; residual must be paid (advance/self-assessment)**.
- **MF dividends / IDCW (Sec 194K)**: TDS 10% above ₹10,000/AMC/FY (from 1-Apr-2025); taxable at slab as IFOS; declare GROSS + claim TDS credit. 194K does NOT apply to redemptions/SWP/switches — those are capital gains events (a **switch = redemption**, taxable even without cash payout).
- Sec 57: only borrowing interest deductible vs dividends, capped 20% of dividends.
- Sources: sahi.com TDS chart FY25-26; tax2win/taxgarden 193; bajajfinservmarkets/busy/taxbuddy 194K; finnovate/sum.money dividend tax guides.

## I. Form 121 (verified 29 Jul 2026 — client asked re WintWealth prompt)
- New unified self-declaration (Section 393(6), IT Act 2025 + Rule 211, IT Rules 2026) **replacing Forms 15G/15H from 1-Apr-2026** (Tax Year 2026-27). Declares: estimated tax on total income = NIL → payer must not deduct TDS. Covers interest, dividends, MF income, rent, insurance commission, PF, etc. Resident individuals/HUF only; PAN mandatory; filed per payer, per year; UIN-tracked & reported to dept.
- **It does NOT make income tax-free — only prevents TDS. Eligibility = NIL total tax liability.**
- **Client verdict: INELIGIBLE (FY26-27 tax ≈ ₹8.6L). Filing it = false declaration → penal consequences. WintWealth prompt = generic marketing, not personalized.**
- Single depository submission (NSDL/CDSL one-form-for-all-bonds) announced Budget 2026, live ~Apr-2027.
